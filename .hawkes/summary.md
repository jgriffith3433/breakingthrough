# Input buffer, dodge cancel and Wallit's hurt reaction

Four commits (nothing compiled here: no Unity compiler in the sandbox; compile once).

1. **Buffer.** `BatEquipment.ReadInput` now starts the swing on the frame of the press (it used to wait for the release) and a press during a swing queues the next combo step; it fires the moment the bat is chainable. A press lost (overwritten while queued, or waiting over 0.6 s) is `Feel.Drop`. `CampaignAbilities` buffers the Dodge press 150 ms; a dodge pressed in a swing's recovery (or while charging) cuts the swing short and goes off at once, one pressed before the contact is held until it. Dodge itself stays campaign-only (AGENTS), so Arcade has the swing buffer only.
2. **Hurt reaction** (`WallitHurt.cs`, from `PlayerGrowth.OnHit`): 1.0 m (1.5 m for a boss, elite or Spider Bot) shove away from the hitter over 0.2 s, 0.6 s window (blows inside it find nothing, climbers wait), white/red outline strobe on the shared Wallit materials, the existing hitReact flinch clip when the bat is not mid-swing (a swing is never taken out of his hands), the existing hurt line (gap 4 s to 1.5 s), and `CameraFeel.HurtJolt` (a small `ZeldaStyleCamera.Shake`) only on heavy hits; Reduce Motion turns it off. TickFight is untouched. AGENTS' shake rule now names this one exception.
3. **Metrics** (`Feel.cs`): feel.inputLatencyMs (from when Wallit could act to the visible response), feel.bufferMs (150), combat.droppedInputs, feel.hitStopMs (from `Juice`, numbers unchanged), plus swing/dodge latency, feel.dodgeOutOfSwingMs, feel.hurtKnockM, feel.hurtWindowS.
4. **`combo-feel`** (named in `.hawkes/scenario.txt`): in Chapter 1's run, a 10-press combo with each press inside the last 150 ms of the swing, a Dodge out of recovery, then a bot hit. Desktop uses mouse and X; at 390x844 fingers tap the Swing and Dodge pads. If no bot reaches Wallit in 25 s a named setup event lands a claw through `PlayerGrowth.OnHit`.

Limits: the flinch is the existing clip, not a new pose, and is skipped mid-swing. Not run. No conflicts. No GPU work, no estimate needed.
