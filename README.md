# Controls

Press **H** in game to open or close this list.

- **W A S D** or the **arrow keys** move. Movement follows the camera, and the character turns to face the direction you are going.
- Hold **F** to jump. This only works in the normal follow camera. It does nothing while locked on or in first person.
- Hold **E** to aim the camera along the direction the character is facing. Release **E** to put the camera back behind the character.
- Press **Q** to enter first person, and press **Q** again to leave. You cannot enter first person while locked on. In first person, **A** and **D** turn left and right, and **W** and **S** look up and down. Letting go of **W** or **S** levels the view. You do not walk in this view.
- Press **Space** to lock onto the nearest target, if there is one. Press **Space** again to drop the lock. The lock also drops if you get too far from the target. While locked on, **W A S D** still move you, and the character keeps facing the target.
- Standing still for about two seconds makes the camera circle on its own. Walking puts it back behind you.
